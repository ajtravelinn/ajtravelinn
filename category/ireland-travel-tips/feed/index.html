]]> Are you a bargain hunter or a vintage fan? Then you’ll love car boot sales in Cork! These bustling markets offer a unique opportunity to discover hidden treasures, from antique jewelry to rare vinyl records.

Why Car Boot Sales in Cork?

Cork’s car boot sales are more than just markets; they’re a cultural experience. They offer a glimpse into local life, a chance to meet friendly sellers, and the thrill of the unexpected find. Whether you’re a seasoned shopper or a first-time visitor, you’re sure to have a memorable experience.

Top Car Boot Sales in Cork

Here are some of the most popular car boot sales in Cork:

Kilcully Car Boot Sale:

Location: Kilcully, Co. Cork

Admission Fee: €2 for Adult | Children free

Seller Fees: €10 per car

Known for its diverse range of goods, from vintage clothing to antique furniture.

Facilities: Plenty of parking spaces, toilet facilities, and refreshments available

Kilcully Scout Camp is a fantastic spot for both sellers and buyers. The atmosphere is always lively, and with a wide range of items up for grabs, it’s easy to spend a few hours exploring.

Ballydehob Car Boot Sale:

Location: Ballydehob, Co. Cork

Admission Fee: Free

Seller Fees: €10 per car

Facilities: Indoor and outdoor spaces, toilet facilities, and free refreshments for sellers

Ballydehob’s car boot sale is a hit, especially with its free admission. This sale offers indoor and outdoor stalls, so rain or shine, it’s always open. The community atmosphere here is unbeatable, and you’ll often find a mix of vintage items, handmade goods, and household bargains. A charming seaside town with a vibrant offering of a mix of local crafts, antiques, and collectibles.

Belgooly GAA Car Boot Sale

Location: Belgooly GAA Grounds, Belgooly, Co. Cork

Admission Fees: €2 per adult | children enter free

Seller Fees: €10 per car

Facilities: On-site toilets and plenty of parking space

A popular car boot sale with a friendly atmosphere and a wide variety of goods, including clothing, toys, and household items.

Belgooly is a once-a-month event but well worth the wait. There’s always a variety of items, and the location is convenient for sellers and buyers alike.

Castletownroche Car Boot Sale:

Location: Castletownroche, Co. Cork

Admission Fee: €2 for Adult | Children free

Seller Fees: €10 per car

A historic town with a bustling car boot sale, offering a mix of antiques, collectibles, and local produce.

This historic town’s car boot sale is a great place to find antiques and collectibles.

Rathcormac Car Boot Sale:

Location: Rathcormac, Co. Cork

Admission Fee: €2 per car

Seller Fees: €10 per car

A popular car boot sale with a wide variety of goods, including clothing, toys, and household items.

 I’ve found some great deals on clothing and household items at this car boot sale.

Tips for Car Boot Sales in Cork

  • Arrive Early: The best deals often go quickly, arrive early to get the first pick.
  • Wear Comfortable Shoes: You will be doing a lot of walking, so make sure your shoes are comfortable.
  • Bring Cash: Many sellers prefer cash payments.
  • Bring a Bag or Box: You’ll need something to carry your purchases.
  • Haggle: Don’t hesitate to negotiate for a better price.”
  • Check Items Carefully: Before buying, inspect items carefully for any damage or defects.

Frequently Asked Questions.

1. When are car boot sales in Cork? 

Car boot sales in Cork typically take place on weekends, often on Sundays. However, specific dates and times can vary, so it’s best to check local listings or social media for the most up-to-date information.

2. Where are the best car boot sales in Cork? 

Some of the most popular car boot sales in Cork include:

  • Kilcully Car Boot Sale
  • Ballydehob Car Boot Sale
  • Belgooly Car Boot Sale
  • Castletownroche Car Boot Sale
  • Rathcormac Car Boot Sale

3. What can I find at a car boot sale in Cork? 

You can find a wide variety of items at car boot sales in Cork, including:

  • Vintage clothing and accessories
  • Antiques and collectibles
  • Household items
  • Toys and games
  • Books and CDs
  • Local crafts and produce

4. How much does it cost to enter a car boot sale in Cork? 

The entry fee for car boot sales in Cork is typically around €2 per car.

5. Can I sell items at a car boot sale in Cork? 

Yes, you can sell items at car boot sales in Cork. However, you’ll usually need to pay a fee to the organizers.

6. What is the best time to go to a car boot sale in Cork?

The best time to go to a car boot sale in Cork is early in the morning, as the best deals often go quickly.

Car Boot Sales: A Treasure Trove

I’ve always loved the thrill of the hunt, and car boot sales are the perfect place to indulge in that feeling. You never know what you might find – a rare antique, a vintage piece of clothing, or a unique gift for a loved one.

The best part about car boot sales is the sense of community. You will meet people from all walks of life, share stories, and make new friends. It’s a truly unique and rewarding experience.

So, the next time you’re in Cork, be sure to check out one of the many car boot sales. You might just find your next favorite treasure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the biggest car boot sale in Ireland?  

The biggest car boot sale in Ireland can be found at Fairyhouse Racecourse. It’s massive and packed with a variety of stalls and hidden gems!

What sells most at a car boot?  

From my experience, clothes, toys, and household items always seem to fly off the table first.

What car make sells the most?

Ford and Volkswagen tend to be the top-selling car brands when it comes to second-hand sales at car boots.

How do I arrange my car boot sale? 

It’s easy! Just book a spot at a local car boot sale, load up your car, set up your stall, and be ready to haggle for some good deals.

What items are sold at a car boot sale?

You’ll see everything from old books and DVDs to antiques, furniture, and gadgets. Basically, anything and everything!

How much should I sell stuff for at a car boot sale? 

Prices can vary, but most things go for €1 to €20, depending on their condition and what they are.

What do you keep in the boot of a car?

Most people keep a spare tire, toolkit, and emergency items like a first-aid kit. Handy to have on the go!

Why are car boot sales good?

Car boot sales are a fun way to declutter, make a bit of extra cash, and maybe even find a bargain for yourself.

What sells most at a car boot?

Kids’ toys, clothes, and small electronics seem to be the best sellers at most car boot sales.

How do I arrange my car boot sale?

Just book your spot, gather your stuff, and make sure you arrive early to grab a good space for your stall. And don’t forget to bring change!
