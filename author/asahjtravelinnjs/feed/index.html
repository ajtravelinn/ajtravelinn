Why You Should Travel to Norway?

Norway is a fascinating destination with its famous fjords, Viking legends, rich cultural heritage, and quaint fishing villages. From north to south, it is a nature lover’s paradise boasting numerous trendy cities to explore.

Whether you’re an adventure seeker or want to cuddle up under a natural light in a glass igloo, Norway won’t disappoint.

While visiting this Scandinavian nation you can stroll through its majestic capital city and cruise the miles of coastline. Or drive through mountain roads and hike one or more of its nearly 300 mountain peaks. All this, and more, awaits you on a holiday in Norway.

What else do you want? Thanks to the variety of landscapes, natural phenomena, activities, and ways to get around, Norway makes a great destination all year round.

In summer, the extended daylight hours of the midnight sun are perfect for cruises and road trips. Come in winter and be rewarded with ideal conditions for seeing the northern lights and fluffy snow instead of going dog-sledding.

Walking Around

Norway is one of the most mountainous countries in Europe and also claims the continent’s longest coastline. Then you can imagine why this Scandinavian destination offers incredible excursions by land and sea. There are many ways to explore the different places you want to see.

Travel independently by train and cruise to Norway’s iconic sights on the Nutshell® route between Oslo and Bergen. Here you will combine overland travel, through forests and along steep mountain roads, with fjord cruises.

To see more of Norway from the sea, a coastal cruise offers a relaxing way to enjoy the country’s famous scenery. Board a ship that will take you south from Bergen to Kirkenes inside the Arctic Circle. You will have the opportunity to stop at historic port towns and shore excursions along the way.

Or maybe a road trip is your way of exploring. Take the wheel and you can experience Norway’s scenic coastal highways and zig-zagging mountain roads on an unforgettable journey.

In the capital city, Oslo, you can rely on a comprehensive public transport system consisting of buses, trams, trains, metro (T-Ben) and boats. Tickets and passes can be purchased at various locations in the city, including the information center Oslo-Gardermoen Airport and Trafikenten, located just outside Oslo Central Station.

We also recommend purchasing an Oslo Pass, which includes free travel on the public transport system in addition to discounts on entry to museums and attractions.

Note: Norway’s people are widely spoken in English, so you should have no problem getting around. Learn more about the Norwegian language to learn some useful words before your stay.

Weather and when to visit

There isn’t a bad time to visit Norway, and Nordic Visitor offers tours all year round so you can choose the one that’s right for you.

It all depends on what you want to do and see most when you travel to Norway. Do you like hiking and kayaking? Are you interested in maximizing daylight for driving and sightseeing?

Then May to September is the best time for you to travel. These months enjoy the warmest weather of the year, the longest daylight, and the best driving conditions. Some roads may be closed in winter, so our self-drive tours in Norway are only available during the summer season.

Enjoy Midnight Sun

Are you interested in experiencing the phenomenon called the midnight sun, where the sun never sets below the horizon? Then come late June, or early July, and make sure to head north, as it is the strongest in the Arctic Circle. Don’t forget to bring your sleeping mask!

May to August when you can go on a small guided group tour.

Do you like winter scenery?

November to March is a great time to vacation in the northern part of the country. Because during these months you will have the best chance to see the northern lights of Norway.

In northern Norway, you will experience the polar night in late December, or early January. Unlike the midnight sun, it occurs when the sun does not rise above the horizon. This allows you to see the northern lights with more darkness.

Here, for dog-sledding, snowmobiling, skiing, and other winter sports you’ll also find ideal snow conditions.

In addition, October and April are considered the shoulder seasons in Norway, when accommodations are more available and fewer visitors arrive. It means that you will have more attraction towards yourself. You can see some beautiful fall colors throughout October.

What You should pack for your Norway trip?

As in other northern European countries, it is best to be prepared for any weather in Norway. This means that whatever the season, you need a warm jumper or fleece, a waterproof jacket, and good walking shoes or boots for all types of terrain.

10 cities that Should be visited when traveling to Norway

Discover the heart of Norway by exploring these 10 captivating cities that beckon with a tapestry of history, natural wonders, and cultural richness. Our journey begins in the enchanting city of Bergen, where every cobblestone street whispers tales of centuries gone by.

1. Bergen:

It will not be wrong to call this city Wooden City. Most of the architecture in this city is made of wood. Indeed, this place is different from all other places in the world. It has the famous Seven Hills assembly with 60 historic buildings. Along with fresh seafood, you will find several museums and photo studios here.

2. Lofoten:

This beautiful island is called a masterpiece of nature. This is because this archipelago is a naturally formed long narrow island with one end connected to the mainland. Around it is the sea. It has quite a reputation for fishing. So this city can be quite a favorite place for those who love fishing. The experience of going once to the sea and once to the land from this narrow island is bound to overwhelm you.

3. Tromso:

Have you heard of the Arctic? It means polar region. Tromsø has all the beauty of the polar regions. The region is famous for its city of Tromsavaya. Although it has an urban feel, it has the same arctic beauty. The Polar Center and Polar Museum can be found here. It also has a wire lift facility. These all will help you to have a wonderful adventurous time.

4. Oslo:

Oslo is the capital of Norway. The city is famous for some of the best food and restaurants. The city is famous for its nightlife. Music is organized in various places for entertainment. You can also enjoy this city tour by cycling or walking. In Oslo, you will find the Viking Museum. Wooden boats and tools used by Viking explorers dating back to about 1,200 years ago are preserved here. The museum houses Vikings’ clothing, tools utensils, and various items found in their tombs that will take you back in time.

5. Geirangerfjord:

Geirangerfjord is one of the wonders of nature that will attract you differently. It is 15 km long and surrounded by high and dense green hills. There are various waterfalls here. The height of the peaks here is more than 1000 meters. You can go hiking but it is quite risky. The beautiful villages and mountains like the pictures will surely attract you.

6. Flam

As a child, many people have seen the scene of a beautiful train in various view cards. Flam is as beautiful as the imagination. Flam is a small village in Orlandsfjord, Norway. Pham means imaginary beautiful. So enjoy the beauty of this village and visit Flam by train.

7. Svalbard:

It is called the Kingdom of the Polar Bear. You know that it is one of the most beautiful animals in the world. Go to Svalbard to meet polar bears. You can see polar bears, whales, seals, arctic foxes, deer, etc. in this place in Norway. But they need a tour guide to see them. Tour guides in this country are very friendly. An interesting fact about this place is that it is forbidden to bury dead bodies there. Because the temperature here is so low that dead bodies do not decompose. If you visit Longyearbyan city here, you can bring such interesting information.

8. Trondheim:

Among the various songs of Bollywood, you will see a row of houses of different colors as far as the eye can see. Trondheim is one such place. This city is famous for its rows of colorful houses. The city is also the origin of the live music cafes you see today. The view of this city will give you a romantic atmosphere. There are plenty of cafes and restaurants around to taste the traditional local cuisine of Trøndelag province.

9. Stavanger

This city is known as the city of natural height. This coastal city has supplied Norway with fuel for almost 50 years. This city is well known for hiking. However, you can find buildings of various colors here. There are two well-named plateaus, Lyfjord and Prykestolen. You can visit them with time.

10. North Cape

This is a unique beautiful place. If you could see the sun in the middle of the night, what kind of surprise would you think? It’s three o’clock in the morning in the house, but the sun shines in the sky! Yes, that is possible in Norway’s Nordekop. The sun never sets here from May 18 to July 29. What is more interesting is that it is about 1000 feet above the Arctic Ocean. As a result, if you can climb here, then you will get the experience of climbing to the top of the world. More than 2 lakh pilgrims visit here every year. It is a hub in Europe where international roads meet.

Top 10 Destination Ideas for Your First Trip Together

Now that the goal of the first trip with your partner is set, let’s discuss where to go because it is most important for any journey. Here are the top 10 destination ideas that promise an unforgettable first trip together as a couple:

Paris, France:

Paris, often referred to as the “City of Love,” exudes an unparalleled romantic ambiance. Here the Eiffel Tower which is well-known as an iconic tower over the world stands as a timeless symbol of the city of Paris. It offers stunning views of the skyline. From historic cathedrals like Notre Dame to artistic districts like Montmartre, the streets of Paris are lined with charming architecture. Wander the neighborhood streets lined with cobblestone streets lined with cafes and patisseries, where couples can enjoy great pastries and people-watching. The Seine River flows gracefully through the heart of the city. It offers visitors boat cruises that pass by landmarks such as the Louvre Museum and the Musée d’Orsay. The scene of Paris’s rich culture includes world-class art galleries, theaters, and music venues. These elements add to its appeal as a charming destination for couples.

Santorini, Greece:

Santorini boasts a surreal beauty characterized by its whitewashed buildings set against the crystal-clear waters of the Aegean Sea. Sunsets in Santorini are legendary, casting a golden glow over the iconic blue-domed churches and picturesque villages perched on cliffs. The island’s volcanic origins have shaped its rugged landscapes, offering unique beaches with sands of various colors, from black to red. Exploring the charming streets of Oia and Fira reveals stunning views and local boutiques. Couples can indulge in exquisite Greek cuisine at seaside tavernas, relax in luxurious resorts with private infinity pools, and immerse themselves in the tranquil island atmosphere.

Venice, Italy:

Venice is a masterpiece of canals and bridges, captivating couples with its romantic charm. Gondolas navigate the waterways, allowing partners to glide beneath ancient arches and past stunning architecture. St. Mark’s Square is a cultural hub, surrounded by historic sites like St. Mark’s Basilica and the Doge’s Palace. Getting lost in the narrow alleys and crossing the Rialto Bridge offers intimate moments of discovery. The Venetian islands, including Murano and Burano, showcase artisanal glassblowing and colorful houses. Couples can savor Venetian cuisine at waterfront restaurants, enjoying fresh seafood and fine Italian wines.

Kyoto, Japan:

Kyoto is a serene escape into traditional Japanese culture. The city is adorned with exquisite temples and shrines, such as the iconic Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Fushimi Inari Taisha with its vibrant torii gates. Kyoto’s immaculately maintained gardens, like the peaceful Kiyomizu-dera, offer tranquil spaces for couples to stroll hand in hand. The Gion district embodies old-world charm, where geisha and traditional tea houses still exist. Cherry blossoms in spring and vibrant autumn foliage add bursts of color to the city’s scenery. Exploring the Nishiki Market and enjoying authentic kaiseki meals provide opportunities to savor Kyoto’s culinary delights.

Bali, Indonesia:

Bali enchants with its tropical paradise setting. Lush rice terraces and dense jungles create a backdrop for exploration and adventure. Couples can relax on pristine beaches, including the famous Uluwatu and Seminyak beaches. Ubud is a cultural center with art markets, traditional dance performances, and spiritual experiences. Temples like Uluwatu Temple stand atop dramatic cliffs overlooking the ocean. Bali’s luxury resorts offer private villas, infinity pools, and rejuvenating spa treatments. The island’s vibrant nightlife and beachfront dining options ensure couples have diverse experiences.

Sedona, Arizona, USA:

Sedona’s red rock formations create a captivating desert landscape. Couples can hike through canyons, such as Bell Rock and Cathedral Rock, for panoramic views and spiritual connection. The town’s New Age atmosphere is complemented by art galleries and wellness retreats. Jeep tours offer thrilling exploration of the rugged terrain, and hot air balloon rides provide an elevated perspective of the surroundings. Sedona’s sunsets cast a warm glow over the sandstone formations, creating an enchanting atmosphere.

Prague, Czech Republic:

Prague’s fairytale charm is evident in its medieval architecture and cobblestone streets. The Prague Castle complex dominates the skyline, while the Charles Bridge connects historic districts. Strolling through Old Town Square transports couples back in time with its stunning astronomical clock and Gothic churches. Prague’s art scene is vibrant, with the Alfons Mucha Museum and the Prague National Gallery showcasing diverse collections. Enjoying a cruise along the Vltava River provides a unique perspective of the city’s beauty.

Cinque Terre, Italy:

Cinque Terre presents a string of colorful villages nestled along the rugged Ligurian coastline. The villages—Monterosso al Mare, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola, and Riomaggiore—are connected by scenic hiking trails, offering stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea. Couples can explore charming streets, enjoy fresh seafood by the waterfront, and relax on rocky beaches. The picturesque landscapes and quaint architecture make Cinque Terre a painter’s paradise.

Bora Bora, French Polynesia:

Bora Bora is the epitome of tropical luxury. Overwater bungalows provide direct access to clear turquoise waters, offering snorkeling and diving opportunities to explore vibrant coral reefs. Couples can relax on private beaches, indulge in spa treatments with a view, and savor Polynesian cuisine. For a romantic getaway, the island’s pristine lagoons and lush greenery create a beautiful setting.

New York City, USA:

New York City’s energy and diversity make it an exciting destination for couples. For exploration Iconic landmarks such as the Statue of Liberty, Times Square, and Central Park provide ample opportunities. Broadway shows and world-class museums like the Metropolitan Museum of Art offer cultural experiences. Chelsea and Greenwich Village are vibrant areas of the city that offer unique atmospheres and culinary delights. Walking through the bustling streets and admiring the city lights from skyscrapers create unforgettable moments.

For a memorable first trip together each of these destinations offers a unique beauty and experience for couples.
