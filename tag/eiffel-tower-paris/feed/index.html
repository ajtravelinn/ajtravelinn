The Eiffel Tower

In 1889 Mr. Gustav built the Eiffel Tower. There were 18 thousand 38 metal parts of iron or steel to make the whole tower. The Eiffel Tower stands on four massive iron pillars. Around 250 construction workers or laborers completed the construction work uninterruptedly for 2 years and 5 months. How wonderful it is to see this scene at night cannot be understood without seeing it with your own eyes.

Some of the artworks here, especially the sculptures, are eye-catching! Seeing some sculptures, people may think wrong! This place is so vast that it will take two days to explore the whole place.
